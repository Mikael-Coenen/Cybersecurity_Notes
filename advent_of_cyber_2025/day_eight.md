Main traits of LLM's:
    - Text generation
    - Stored knowledge
    - Follow instructions
- Since LLMs follow text patterns they can be tricked
- Attackers can shape prompts to make LLMs produce reponses that are unsafe
- Agentic AI: AI with agency capabilities (not restricted by narrow instructions)
- Chain of thought (CoT): prompt engineering method designed to improve the reasoning capabilities of LLMs
    - Since to access to external resources it can hallucinate facts
- ReAct: Reason and action unified to address limitations of CoT (ex: reasons and searches google for answers)

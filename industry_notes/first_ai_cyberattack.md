- First AI cyberattack was by a Chinese-state sponsored group called GTG-1002
- Used Claude Code AI to attack thirty global targets
- Claude automated 80-90% of the attack, with the attackers only intervening on a few critical decisions
- Attackers jail-broke Claude by tricking Claude into thinking they were a cybersecurity firm doing normal defensive testing
- Claude was able to identify highest value databases and highest priviliged accounts
- Industry needs to look towards adopting defensive AI cybersecurity capabilities
